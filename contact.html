FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Have some questions before joining? Here are some of the most common ones we receive.

What is FieldForm?
FieldForm is a local food directory designed to connect shoppers with independent food producers like organic farmers, bakers, and confectioners.
How much does it cost to join?
Joining the directory as a producer is currently free for our beta launch. We want to help as many independent makers as possible.
Who can be listed on the directory?
We welcome organic farms, artisan bakeries, and small-batch confectioners who are committed to transparency and quality ingredients.
How do customers contact me?
Customers can reach out to you directly through the contact information provided on your dedicated profile page.
Can I update my profile after it's live?
Yes! You can email our team at any time to update your photos, product offerings, or operating hours.
